# Block Combo — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 21, 2026_

## Overview

Block Combo is an offline puzzle game. You do not need an account, and we do
not ask for your name, email, contacts, location or any other personal
information.

## Data stored on your device

The game saves your best score, sound and haptics preferences, and basic play
counters (games played, app launches) locally on your device. This data never
leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Advertising

When ads are enabled, they are served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect
device information and advertising identifiers to show and measure ads, as
described in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
and [AdMob policies](https://support.google.com/admob). You can reset or
limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

## Analytics

The current version does not send analytics to any server. If a future
version adds analytics (e.g. Firebase) to improve the game, this policy will
be updated first.

## Children

Block Combo is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be shipped with the app
update and published at the policy URL on the store listing.
