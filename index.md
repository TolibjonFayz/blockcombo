# Block Combo — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 16, 2026_

## Overview

Block Combo is an offline puzzle game. You do not need an account, and we do
not ask for your name, email, contacts, location or any other personal
information.

## Data stored on your device

The game saves your best score, daily streak, sound and haptics preferences,
and basic play counters locally on your device. This data is deleted when you
uninstall the app.

## Advertising

Ads are served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information and
advertising identifiers to show and measure ads, as described in
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can reset
or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

## Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to learn how the game is played (for
example, when a game starts or ends and which features are used) so we can
improve it. It collects an app instance identifier, device type and these
gameplay events — never your name, email or precise location. See
[how Google uses data](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Feedback

If you send feedback, your email app sends us your message, your email address
and the device model and Android version shown in the draft. We use them only
to reply and fix problems.

## Children

Block Combo is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be shipped with the app
update and published at <https://tolibjonfayz.github.io/blockcombo/>.
